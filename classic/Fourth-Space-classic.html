A SHORT FIELD GUIDE

W is a direction,
not a destination.

You know three independent spatial directions. Here, W is a fourth: moving in W does not move you in X, Y or Z.

  1. Read the exact slice. The main map shows two axes. The other two are held at your current coordinates. Change “View plane” to put W directly on the map.
  2. Follow the passages. Bright movement buttons are open. A dot is a room; a line joins adjacent rooms. Badges on your room show exits outside the displayed plane.
  3. Step into W. In the first maze, the barrier ends in W. Step out of its slice, walk around it, then step back. Some barriers can span multiple W slices.
  4. Reach all four coordinates. Matching the exit’s X and Y is not enough. Z and W must match, too.

“Ana” and “kata” are traditional names for opposite fourth-dimensional directions. This is an intuition-building prototype, not a validated cognitive training program. The projection is not a literal view of 4D space.
